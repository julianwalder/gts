Integrated Pilot Training Systems

De-risk pilot training from pre-selection to mission debriefing

Train pilots using a high-fidelity twin of the Grob G120TP across the entire training pipeline — from pilot screening and cockpit familiarization to advanced simulation and mission analysis.

The Grob training ecosystem replicates the cockpit architecture and procedures of the Grob G120TP across multiple training devices. From mixed-reality cockpit trainers to advanced dome simulators, the system supports pilot training from candidate pre-selection through mission planning, simulation and post-mission debriefing.

The ecosystem integrates:

  • Type-specific simulators
  • Mission planning tools
  • Operational training environments
  • Briefing and debriefing systems

This creates a continuous aircraft-specific training environment connecting simulation and aircraft operations.
