Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need
to Know Before Ordering

What exactly is included in the conversion kit? +
The kit is a comprehensive "firewall-forward" solution including the Rotax 912iSc3 engine, engine mount, vibration dampers, unfeathering accumulator, and a new engine cowling featuring an integrated high-power LED landing light.
How does engine handling change compared to the original Grob engine? +
Handling is significantly easier. Manual magneto checks are replaced by a simple ECU channel check (Channel A/B) performed at 4,000 RPM. Additionally, the Rotax engine rotates clockwise, meaning directional control is maintained with right rudder during the take-off run, unlike the original configuration.
What are the fuel and oil requirements for the new engine? +
The Rotax engine is highly flexible. It is approved for EN 228 Super/Super Plus and Super E10. While AVGAS 100LL is permitted, please note that it increases stress on valve seats and requires reduced oil change intervals. Approved oil must meet Rotax Service Instruction SI-912 i-001 (RON 424).
What is the estimated lead time? +
The estimated delivery time is approximately 10 months, as propellers are manufactured on order. We recommend placing your order early to secure your preferred delivery slot for 2026.
How is the weight and balance affected after the conversion? +
While the conversion results in a 20 kg weight saving, a new aircraft weighing must be performed after installation to determine the new Center of Gravity (CG) and empty weight, which must then be recorded in your updated Aircraft Flight Manual.
