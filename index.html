Type-Specific Design

A high-fidelity twin of the Grob G120TP

All Grob training devices are based on the Grob G120TP. Across the ecosystem, pilots train with the same cockpit logic, avionics structure and procedural architecture as the aircraft platform.

This type-specific approach supports:

  • earlier cockpit familiarity
  • stronger procedural consistency
  • more efficient transfer from simulation to flight
  • a closer connection between training and operations
Type-Specific Architecture

Every device is built around the Grob G120TP cockpit environment.

Consistent Cockpit Logic

Consistent avionics, controls and procedures across the training system.

Continuous Learning Path

Training progresses through multiple devices without changing aircraft logic.

Stronger Transfer to Flight

Simulation remains closely aligned with aircraft operations and mission preparation.

Operational Environment

Train in your operational environment

Beyond cockpit procedures, the training ecosystem supports mission-oriented preparation in environments aligned with real operations. The system can be configured to reflect:

  • operating airfields
  • terrain and surrounding geography
  • airspace structure
  • routes and operational procedures
  • mission scenarios

This allows training organizations to move from procedural instruction toward operational preparation within a type-specific training environment.
